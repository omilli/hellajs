## API

A drag-dismissable panel mounting on one of the four screen edges: the caller owns an `open` accessor and an `onClose` callback, the panel follows the pointer while dragged, and release past a distance or velocity threshold dismisses it.

Opening portals the overlay and panel into `document.body` under `data-state="open"`; closing keeps the panel mounted until its exit slide ends, with the dismissal wirings down while it leaves. Escape and outside pointerdown route through the same `onClose`, and the focus trap stays armed the whole time the drawer rests open.

The composed `Drawer` ships beside four content-vocabulary components: `DrawerHeader`, `DrawerFooter`, `DrawerTitle`, and `DrawerDescription` compose inside the root's children slot.

### Drawer

| Prop | Type | Default |
| ---- | ---- | ------- |
| `open` | `Signal<boolean>` | `-` |
| `onClose` | `() => void` | `-` |
| `direction` | `"top" \| "right" \| "bottom" \| "left"` | `"bottom"` |
| `title` | `string` | `-` |
| `description` | `string` | `-` |
| `closeOnEscape` | `boolean` | `true` |
| `closeOnOutside` | `boolean` | `true` |
| `class` | `string` | `-` |
| `children` | `HellaChildren` | `-` |
Accepts all native `div` attributes (forwarded to the root element).

`direction` is the edge the panel mounts on; it sets `data-vaul-drawer-direction` and the dismissal axis. `title` renders as a wired `DrawerTitle` (the panel's `aria-labelledby` target) and `description` as a wired `DrawerDescription` (the `aria-describedby` target).

The heading: an `h2` carrying `data-slot="drawer-title"` whose `id` the panel's `aria-labelledby` points at.

The supporting line: a `p` carrying `data-slot="drawer-description"` whose `id` the panel's `aria-describedby` points at.

Every component also takes a `class` string, landing last in its style composition.

## Drag Dismissal

The whole panel is a drag surface (the grip handle at the top of a bottom drawer included): the panel's transform follows the pointer live along its dismissal axis, and the overlay's opacity fades in proportion to how far the panel has traveled. Release dismisses past either threshold:

| Threshold | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Distance | 25% of the panel size along the dismissal axis | Released past this fraction of the way out dismisses |
| Velocity | 500px/s toward the edge at release | A flick dismisses no matter how short the drag |

Below both thresholds the panel springs back through the restored transform transition - the same transition carrying the enter and exit slides, so open, close, and drag all move through one mechanism. Above either one the panel receives its off-edge transform, `onClose` fires, and the panel unmounts when the exit slide ends.

<Callout variant="info">Interactive elements opt out of the drag: pointerdowns on buttons, links, inputs, or anything marked `data-no-drag` never move the panel.</Callout>

## Direction

`direction` picks the edge the panel mounts on and carries both the geometry and the slide direction:

| Direction | Geometry |
|---|---|
| `"bottom"` (default) | Grip handle, top margin, rounded top corners, top border |
| `"top"` | Mirrors the bottom treatment: rounded bottom corners, bottom border |
| `"left"` / `"right"` | Full-height side panel, three-quarters wide capped at 24rem from wide screens up |
